Coaching the Coach+self-improving agents

Does a self-improving coach agent (HyperAgents) train better ARC-AGI-2 solvers? No, and thinking longer beats it.
  • A meta agent rewrites a puzzle-solving agent's code, and is also allowed to rewrite its own. We froze the self-improved coaches and had each train a fresh player on new practice puzzles.
  • On a held-out exam, coached players scored about the same as an uncoached one (29.3%). Raising the model's reasoning effort one notch (41.1%) beat every form of coaching.
  • Interactive explorer: try the puzzles, replay every exam answer, read every coaching session and code change.

ShortcutHunt+under review

Nothing Found, Nothing Checked: testing LLM judges on research agents' null results against planted ground truth.
  • Agents audit linear probes on Gemma-2-2B for a planted shortcut, like British spelling. Some probes have none, so "nothing found" can be right, and a program grades every answer.
  • In 254 rollouts by five agents, most errors on the hardest probes (77 of 115) are incorrect nulls: "nothing found" when a shortcut exists.
  • Without ground truth, the main judge (GPT-6.1 Terra) credits 50 of those 77 given the agent's messages, and 39 given the full transcript. It is consistent but agrees only weakly with ground truth (κ = 0.32–0.46), and a 0.86 accuracy gap between two agents shrinks to 0.03.
  • Stricter prompts stop crediting wrong answers only by rejecting correct ones too.

Under review at the AAAI 2027 Workshop on Effective and Efficient Evaluation for AI Scientists.

EditHunt+agent env · interp

Can an AI agent find and undo a secret edit inside a model, without the original?
  • A hidden, self-gating edit is planted in Qwen2.5-1.5B. Patching the wrong answers back recovers at most 0.43 of the original model; finding and inverting the edit recovers up to 0.99, so the task requires looking inside.
  • With no method hint, gpt-6.1-sol worked out model diffing by itself (per-layer activation jumps of affected vs control cities), estimated the plant at cos 0.93–0.97 and passed 5/24 runs, its best in 10 turns for ~$0.07. gpt-6-luna passed 0/8.
  • No run undid a two-edit model (0/8): agents fix one edit and stop looking.
  • Built on a finding that holds in four models: a "state" direction at the city token that works until a fixed handoff layer.
